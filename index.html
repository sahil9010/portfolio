Building trust through

I'm a Quality Assurance engineer based in Kathmandu, Nepal, passionate about ensuring software works exactly as intended — even at its edges. My approach blends systematic test planning with a developer's mindset, letting me uncover bugs before they ever reach real users.

With hands-on experience across Black Box, White Box, and Gray Box testing, I've validated everything from payment gateways and email verification SaaS platforms to AI-powered chatbots, voice assistant applications, and CRM systems. I specialize in testing conversational AI interfaces, validating natural language processing accuracy, and ensuring voice AI quality across diverse use cases.

Beyond functional testing, I build automation frameworks with Playwright and Selenium, run API test suites in Postman, conduct load testing with Apache JMeter, and design comprehensive test strategies for AI/ML systems — translating manual coverage into reliable, repeatable pipelines that keep release cycles short and quality high.
